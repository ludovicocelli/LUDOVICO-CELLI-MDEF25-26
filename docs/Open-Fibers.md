## Open Fibers ##



Every year the textile industry generates over 90 million tonnes of waste, a number so big that is hard to fully grasp. To put it into perspective, this equals to one garbage truck full of clothes dumped or worst  burned every second in a landfill; furthermore, rich countries export massive amounts of second-hand clothing to Asia and Africa, hiding a trash dumping practice under the fake name of helping.
Open Fibers aims to give a solution to this huge amount of waste by creating an easily replicable and low-tech system to shred fabric and mold it into bricks that can then be joined and turned into useful objects in critical contexts of need.
