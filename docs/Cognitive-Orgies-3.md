**Cognitive orgies: Personal reflections**

This orgy was quite different than the previous ones; the fact that we were asked to build a performance around the concepts that we're dealing with in our project was very new and this was both a good and a "bad" thing under my point of view. The nice part was that I had the chance to approach a little differently to the topic I've been dealing with for quite a long time. Usually I consider and work a lot for my project to be the most efficient and effective possible, considering a lot of variables that improve its feasibility and its outcome and trying to apply every small change that could improve the final idea; in this case, with this project being a bit more of an artistic performance than a project with a clear and supposedly efficient outcome I enjoyed the facti that the direction of the project could take off a  little of the pressure that usually I have, about delivering something efficient and perfectly sound. With this I don't wanna say that the project was very fast and easy to build, because it still required us long and intensive work throughout the week, but it felt a bit lighter than usual and this made me enjoy the process while working; this mood allowed the development of the project to be lighter and in some parts funnier; it's been a lot of fun for example to think and design the interaction between the robotic priest and the users, imagining the reaction of the users and in general designing this weird but funny relationship between a pre-programmed robot and a human being that was gonna be unaware of what could happen. On the other hand, I felt a bit frustrated working a lot for something that I kinda knew I wouldn't be able to use explicetely for my final project that was approaching quite fast. We worked a lot setting up the room in order to make it functional for our performance, being an intimate and dark space, allowing both for a more confessional lookalike environment and allowing for projection in the background thanks to the canvas we set up. It also took a lot of time to perfect the disappearing method into the bowl of oil, as it took a lot to write all the cards corresponding to environmental sins and designing the interaction with the robotic priest with coding. But after all of this I felt a bit we were left with almost nothing. As the project was designed to be existing only when being shared and in contact with the "users", after the performance moments were gone, I couldn't use what we built to implement my actual project or to complete my research somehow; I think this is what made me feel a bit frustrated at some points, but I also know that not every thing I do has a clear and direct purpose or outcome individually, but could spark some reflections further in time and positively affect parts of my project that know I'm not able to see.
